OLASUBOMI ELEGANCE WEBSITE PREVIEW

1. Open index.html in Chrome to preview the website.
2. Scroll down to “Store Owner – Add Products”. Enter product name, category, price, details, description, and choose a product photo. Press Add Product.
3. Products are saved in the browser on that device for this prototype.

IMPORTANT FOR A REAL LIVE STORE:
This prototype is a front-end preview. Browser storage does not sync product uploads to other customers or devices. For real online product management, connect the site to a backend/database and cloud image storage (for example Supabase) or use a managed ecommerce platform such as Shopify/WooCommerce. You will also need hosting and a domain name if desired.
